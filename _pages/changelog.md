# Changelog

### `Initial Release`
# **Version 1.0**
**2026**

Welcome to **Caméléon**! Slide the chameleon through the maze and find the way out.

#### Features

- 🦎 **Slide to the Exit** — One swipe, and the chameleon slides until something stops it
- 🎨 **Change Color** — Step in a puddle to take its color; only the doors of your color open
- 🕹️ **100 Levels in 5 Packs** — Neon jungle, retro desert, crystal cave, night forest and volcano
- 🐍 **Traps That Move** — Skulls, fireballs, snakes, spotlights, vines and a dragon
- ⭐ **Three Stars per Level** — Collect every star on your way
