# Privacy Policy

At **Caméléon**, your privacy is our top priority.

<br>

## 1.0 What Stays on Your Device

Your progress (the levels you completed and the stars you earned) and your settings (sounds, music, vibrations) are stored only on your device.

<br>

## 2.0 Advertising

Caméléon is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**: a full-screen ad between two levels. Buying "Remove ads" removes them.

- Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad. You can change it at any time with **Ad choices** in the game's settings.  
- iOS then asks whether the App may track you. If you refuse, ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can change this choice in the Settings app of your device.  
- Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), your IP address, your approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.  

Google's handling of this data is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

<br>

## 3.0 What We Don't Do

- ❌ We don't collect your name, email or contacts  
- ❌ We don't collect usage statistics  
- ❌ We don't track you across other apps or websites without your permission  
- ❌ We don't sell any data  
- ❌ We don't require an account  

<br>

## 4.0 In-App Purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

<br>

## 5.0 Other Permissions

Caméléon does not request access to your microphone, location, photos, contacts, calendar or camera.

<br>

## 6.0 Children's Privacy

Caméléon does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App contains ads.

<br>

## 7.0 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
